          How Docket works

          Docket is your daily record of work done, in a single file. No installs, no server, no account — everything lives in this HTML file and saves automatically on your machine. Not sure what a button does? Hover it — or Tab to it — and it explains itself.

          ● The daily log

          • Type what you did in the box and press Enter — it's saved instantly.
          • The tiles above the log count the open day, its week and its month; the small bars chart the last 14 days — click a bar to jump to that day.
          • Move between days with the ‹ › arrows, or click the big date to open a calendar.
          • Hover an entry and click the ✎ pencil to edit it (Save/Cancel), or ✕ to delete it.
          • Deleted the wrong thing? An Undo button pops up for a few seconds — same for Clear this day.
          • Recent Days in the sidebar lists every day that has entries, grouped by month — click a month name to fold or unfold it, and the ▦ icon opens that month in the calendar.

          ● Copy & paste

          • Copy Day to Clipboard copies the date plus a bulleted list — ready to paste into an email, timesheet or invoice.
          • Copy Items Only copies just the lines, no date heading.

          ● Day · Week · Month views

          • Switch views with the Day / Week / Month tabs. The ‹ › arrows then step by day, week or month.
          • Week shows the whole week's entries at a glance; Month is a calendar with counts per day.
          • Click any day in either view to open it. The copy buttons follow the view — in Month view, Copy Month gives you the full month-to-date list, ready to paste.

          ● Keyboard shortcuts

          They work whenever you're not typing in a box:

          • ← → — previous / next day (steps by week or month in those views)
          • T — jump to today  ·  D W M — switch to Day, Week or Month view
          • N — start typing a new entry  ·  C — copy the open day, week or month
          • ? — open this guide  ·  Esc — close it

          ● Wishlist

          • Queue things that still need doing in the sidebar's Wishlist.
          • When one gets done, drag it onto the log or onto a day — in Recent Days, or straight onto a Week or Month cell. It becomes an entry there.
          • Or hover it and click ↳ to log it on the day you have open. Click its text to edit, ✕ to remove.

          ● Recurring tasks

          • Add tasks you do again and again to Recurring in the sidebar.
          • Drag one onto any day (or click ↳ for the open day) to log it — it stays in the list so you can use it next time.
          • If it's already on that day, Docket skips the duplicate and tells you.

          ● CSV import / export (Excel)

          Bulk-load the month to date from a spreadsheet. One entry per row: date first, task second.

          Date,Task
2026-07-01,"Installed blinds, lounge"
02/07/2026,Quoted new office fit-out
          • The header row is optional. Dates can be 2026-07-01 or 01/07/2026 style (day-first).
          • Comma, semicolon or tab delimited — detected automatically.
          • Re-importing is safe: duplicates and unreadable rows are skipped and reported.
          • Export CSV writes the same format, so you can export → edit in Excel → import back.

          ● Backups & where your data lives

          • Data is stored by this browser, on this computer. Stick to one browser (e.g. always Edge).
          • Export JSON saves everything — including the wishlist — to a file; Import JSON merges it back in. Use it to move to another browser or PC, or as a safety net.
          • Clearing the browser's site data erases the log — keep an occasional export.
          Tip: right-click the Docket.html file → Send to → Desktop (create shortcut) so it's always one click away. Reopen this guide any time with the ? button, top right.
